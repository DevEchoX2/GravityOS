# Privacy Policy for GravityOS
 **Last Updated**: March 10, 2026

At **GravityOS**, we believe in simplicity and transparency. Because GravityOS acts as a proxy, we want to be very clear about what data we process and how we handle it.

# 1. What Data Do We Collect?
We aim to collect as little data as possible.

-** Log Data**: When you use our site, our servers may automatically record information such as your IP address, browser type, and the date/time of your request. This is standard for web servers and is used to maintain security and site functionality.

-** Cookies:** We may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences or improve site performance.

- **User-Provided Information:** If you choose to contact us via email or support channels, we will collect the information you voluntarily provide to us for the purpose of resolving your inquiry.

# 2. How Do We Use This Data?
We use the information we collect to:

- Operate, maintain, and provide the features of GravityOS.

- Protect against unauthorized access, hacking, or fraudulent use of the site.

- Improve our services and user experience.

# 3. How Do We Share Information?
We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

- Legal Requirements: If we are required to do so by law, regulation, or a valid legal request (such as a court order).

- Service Providers: We may work with third-party service providers (like hosting providers) who assist us in running our infrastructure. These parties are contractually obligated to protect your data.

# 4. Third-Party Content
GravityOS is a proxy service that allows you to access third-party websites. Please be aware that:

- When you access a site through GravityOS, that third-party site may collect its own data according to their specific privacy policies.

- We do not control the privacy practices of the websites you visit through our proxy. Please review the privacy policies of any site you navigate to.

# 5. Security
We take reasonable measures to secure our infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

# 6. Children’s Privacy
GravityOS is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete that data.

# 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
